---
title: "When is a claim worth considering as 'maybe fact'?"
tags: ["ctrw", "homework"]
created: 2026-08-19
---

Claim usually contains uncertainty and subjectivity to it. However, a claim can be worth considered as a fact if the person making the statement talks about the universal truth that no one can deny.

Thousands of politicians, musicians, authors, and even teachers have used those universal truths to convince and even compel the listeners that what they claim is true.
