At the moment, Mark Zuckerberg is confused about the gap between his original intention in creating meta, and actual conflict occurring by the opinions of millions of people.

To sought this out, he plans to execute three changes to platforms of Instagram, Facebook, and Thread: end third-party fact checking program (move to community notes), allow more speech, and resume the original approach to political contents. These are the fields that have been mechanized to minimize certain issues through complex systems. However, those complex systems were the ones that made mistakes, affecting millions of people even by the slightest error.
