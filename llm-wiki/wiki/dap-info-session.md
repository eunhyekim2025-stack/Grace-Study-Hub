> [!summary] Key takeaways
> - DAP runs a 12‑week, community‑focused ML program with four pillars: co‑learning, project‑based work, mentorship, senior associate roles.  
> - Application window is 10 days; after a short Hackerrank test you attend Super Day (wk 5) to form a 3‑person team and pitch a project.  
> - Standard project pipeline: data → split → train → evaluate → deploy; a usable dataset is mandatory.  
> - Full‑time participation (Mon‑Thu) is required; rewards include hardware, budget support, and top‑3 project awards.  
> - Resources (slides, code, datasets) are open‑sourced; mentors help with dataset selection and technical guidance.

## Overview
- Mission: build a co‑learning community around machine learning for any faculty.  
- Open‑source all materials; external partners (Cardio, Cargill) provide real‑world projects.  
- Runs only in Semester 2; this is the 12th sustainable iteration.

## Program Structure (Four Pillars)
| Pillar | What you do |
|--------|-------------|
| Co‑learning | Weekly ML topic (e.g., NLP) → participants teach peers; labs & hands‑on exercises. |
| Project‑based | Small groups (3‑5) create a resume‑worthy ML project; focus on impact, not complexity. |
| Mentorship | Each associate paired with a mentor for guidance on model selection, debugging, and project scope. |
| Senior Data Associate | Lead external‑partner projects, metric‑driven work; passes knowledge to next batch. |

> [!info] **Co‑learning** emphasizes intuition over heavy math; mentors are senior students or alumni with 5‑10 years industry experience.

## Timeline & Milestones
- **Weeks 1‑12**: weekly coding sessions, pre‑assigned groups/mentors, one dedicated hack day.  
- **Week 5**: Super Day – networking, project idea brainstorming, team formation (3 members per team).  
- **Weeks 6‑12**: develop project, attend office‑hours (“pejabat terbuka”) for feedback.  
- **Week 13 (optional)**: senior‑GAP activities or partner extensions.  
- **By 23 Oct**: project approval, final presentations, awards.

## Application Process
- **Window**: 10 days from info session.  
- **Hackerrank test**: 30‑60 min coding; partial completion is fine, does not disqualify.  
- **Result**: round‑1 assigns you to a Super Day slot (Wednesday or Friday night).  
- **If solo**: organizers will pair you with other solo applicants; you still have three weeks to solidify a team and submit a proposal.

> [!warning] **Commitment** – avoid enrolling in courses that clash with DAP (e.g., C‑M or I‑S pre‑assigned). Reduce other extracurricular load to stay on track.

## Project Workflow & Ideas
> [!example] **Standard workflow**
> 1. Acquire a reliable dataset (public Hugging Face, Tegel, or self‑collected).  
> 2. Explore & clean the data.  
> 3. Split into training, validation, test sets.  
> 4. Train a model (classification, regression, NN, transformer, etc.).  
> 5. Evaluate; iterate if performance is poor.  
> 6. Deploy and prepare a presentation.

> [!example] **Sample project ideas**
> - Singlish profanity filter (text classification).  
> - Predictive analytics for market trends.  
> - Sentiment analysis on e‑commerce reviews.  
> - Face‑detection dash‑cam attention model.  
> - Recommendation system with a custom dataset.  
> - Clustering of geographic hotspots.  
> - Color‑analysis tool for skin‑tone matching.  
> - Reinforcement‑learning agent for a simple game.

## Mentorship & Resources
- Mentors know public datasets (Hugging Face, Tegel) and suggest suitable projects.  
- All slides, readings, and code are hosted at **smubia.com/projects** (maintained by Jehan).  
- Successful projects may receive a dedicated computer and budget via **GoCollect Pro** for compute beyond laptop RAM.  
- Open Q&A via direct messages; quick response on mistakes, data issues, or other concerns.

## Rewards & Commitment
- **Awards**: top‑3 projects, “Best Co‑learning Group,” “Best Associate” – modest competition‑related prizes, not cash.  
- **Support**: hardware, budget, mentorship, open‑source resources.  
- **Attendance**: full‑time SEM 2, Monday‑Thursday, plus mandatory hack day on a break day.  
- Weekly check‑ins on the first day of each week to coordinate trips and DAP activities.

## Tips for Success
- Bring a **usable dataset** and a clear project plan to the interview – they weigh heavily.  
- Focus on **project appeal**; HR cares more about impact than algorithmic depth.  
- Use Super Day to network, recruit teammates, and refine ideas with mentor feedback.  
- Leverage office hours for proposal polishing; you have three weeks before the final submission deadline.

## Key terms
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| DAP | Data Associate Program – 12‑week ML learning & project cohort. |
| Super Day | Mid‑semester networking event to form teams and pitch project ideas. |
| Senior Data Associate | Role that leads external‑partner projects and mentors newer associates. |
| Hackerrank assessment | Short coding test in the application; not a barrier. |
| GoCollect Pro | Program that provides hardware and budget support for compute‑intensive projects. |
