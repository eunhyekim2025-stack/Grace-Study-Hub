> [!summary] Key takeaways
> - Four roots (CM, Cost Eq., POHR, EU) generate all CVP & costing formulas.  
> - Separate axes: product vs period **and** variable vs fixed.  
> - Decision relevance: ignore sunk, add opportunity, compare differential costs.  
> - High‑low uses activity, not cost, to estimate a linear cost equation.  
> - DOL = CM ÷ Operating Income; amplifies profit change from sales change.

## Cost Classifications & Terminology

> [!info] **Product vs Period Cost** – Product costs stay in inventory until sale; period costs are expensed when incurred.  
> [!info] **Variable vs Fixed** – Variable changes with activity; fixed stays constant within the relevant range.  
> [!warning] **Relevant Range** – Linear cost assumptions hold only inside the activity band where fixed costs truly stay flat.

- **Direct Materials (DM)**, **Direct Labour (DL)**, **Manufacturing Overhead (MO)** = **Cost Trinity**; MO is the hardest to allocate.  
- **Prime Cost** = DM + DL.  
- **Conversion Cost** = DL + MO.  

## Costing Systems

> [!info] **Job Order Costing** – each job/custom product tracked separately; uses POHR per job.  
> [!info] **Process Costing** – identical units; uses Equivalent Units (EU) for each department.

| Concept | Formula / Note |
|---|---|
| **POHR** | Estimated MO ÷ Estimated allocation base |
| **Equivalent Units** | Physical units × % complete (materials & conversion separate) |
| **Cost per EU** | (Total DM + Total Conversion Cost) ÷ Total EU |

## Cost Behaviour & Estimation

> [!info] **Cost Equation** – Y = a + bX (a = total fixed, b = variable per unit).  
> [!warning] **High‑Low Method** – choose high & low **activity** points only; compute slope *b* then intercept *a*.  
> [!info] **Regression** – uses all data points; most accurate but not tested for computation.

| Method | Data Used | Accuracy |
|---|---|---|
| High‑Low | 2 points (activity) | Rough |
| Scattergraph | Visual fit | Diagnostic |
| Least‑Squares Regression | All points | Best |

## Cost‑Volume‑Profit (CVP) Analysis

> [!info] **Contribution Margin (CM)** – Sales − Variable Costs; funds fixed costs & profit.  
> [!info] **CM Ratio** – CM ÷ Sales (or unit CM ÷ price).  
> [!info] **Break‑Even (units)** – Fixed Expenses ÷ CM per unit.  
> [!info] **Target Profit (units)** – (Fixed + Desired Profit) ÷ CM per unit.  
> [!info] **Margin of Safety** – Current sales − Break‑Even sales.  

| Formula | Use |
|---|---|
| **Profit** = (Unit CM × Q) − Fixed | CVP core |
| **Profit** = (Sales × CM Ratio) − Fixed | When sales value given |
| **Break‑Even %** = Fixed ÷ Total CM | % of current sales needed |
| **Degree of Operating Leverage (DOL)** = CM ÷ Operating Income | Profit sensitivity |

> [!example] If Fixed = £120k, Unit CM = £30, price = £50, target profit = £60k → Required Q = (120k+60k)/30 = 6,000 units.

## Budgeting & Performance Management

> [!info] **Master Budget** – coordinated set of all functional budgets (sales, production, cash, etc.).  
> > [!info] **Inventory Equation** – Beginning + Added − Ending = Ending inventory (applies to raw, WIP, FG).  
> > [!info] **Variance Analysis** – Budget vs. Actual; explains favorable/unfavorable gaps.  
> > [!info] **Responsibility Accounting** – evaluates managers only on controllable costs.

## Decision‑Making Framework

> [!info] **Relevant Costs** – differ between alternatives (use).  
> > **Sunk Cost** – already incurred, ignore.  
> > **Opportunity Cost** – foregone benefit, add.  
> > **Differential (Incremental) Cost** – cost difference, always relevant.

- **After‑Tax Target** → Pre‑tax = Net ÷ (1‑tax rate).  
- **Weighted Average CM** – Σ (sales‑mix % × CM per unit).  

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Contribution Margin (CM) | Sales less variable costs; covers fixed costs & profit |
| Predetermined Overhead Rate (POHR) | Estimated MO ÷ Estimated allocation base |
| Equivalent Units (EU) | Work expressed as completed units (materials & conversion separate) |
| Cost Equation | Y = a + bX (fixed + variable per unit) |
| High‑Low Method | Estimates linear cost using two activity points |
| Break‑Even Point | Volume where profit = 0 |
| Degree of Operating Leverage (DOL) | CM ÷ Operating Income; profit sensitivity |
| Master Budget | Full set of coordinated budgets for a period |
| Variance Analysis | Comparison of budgeted vs. actual results |
| Relevant Cost | Cost that differs between decision alternatives |
