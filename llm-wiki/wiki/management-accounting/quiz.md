**Q1. What is the main purpose of management accounting?**

> The main purpose of management accounting is to provide financial information to managers within an organization to help them make informed decisions. It involves the analysis, interpretation, and presentation of financial data to facilitate planning, control, and decision-making.

**Q2. What is cost accounting?**

> Cost accounting is a method of determining the cost of products or services in a company, which involves the collection, analysis, and reporting of cost data.

**Q3. What is the difference between fixed and variable costs?**

> Fixed costs remain the same even if the level of production or sales changes, while variable costs change in proportion to the level of production or sales.

**Q4. What is the balanced scorecard?**

> The balanced scorecard is a framework for measuring organizational performance from four different perspectives: financial, customer, internal processes, and learning and growth.

**Q5. Why is budgeting important in management accounting?**

> Budgeting is important in management accounting as it enables organizations to plan and control their financial resources, and to allocate resources effectively to achieve their objectives.
