# 📓 Management accounting

> [[index|← Subjects]]

Management accounting is **the accounting that managers inside a company use to make decisions** —
as opposed to the outward reporting that [[m01-intro-management-accounting|Module 1]] contrasts it with.
These notes rework *Managerial Accounting: Comprehensive Study Guide (Modules 1–12)* (see `sources:` above)
around analogies and diagrams, so that someone **encountering the subject for the first time** can follow it;
the whole arc runs [[m02-cost-concepts-job-order-costing|Module 2]] → [[m06-cvp-analysis|Module 6]] → [[m09-12-performance-management|Modules 9–12]].

> [!tip] Switch to **Diagram view** at the top of this page
> Every module is a card there — click one to open the note.

## Where to start

1. **[[m01-intro-management-accounting|Module 1]]** — what kind of subject this actually is
2. **[[m02-cost-concepts-job-order-costing|Module 2]]** — the cost trinity and the factory wall. Everything else roots here
3. **[[m06-cvp-analysis|Module 6]]** — the most practical and most satisfying part; this is where it clicks
4. Then the rest in order

> [!important] How to study the formulas
> Do not brute-force sixteen of them. Fix **Contribution Margin (Sales − Variable Costs)** firmly in mind and
> break-even, target profit, margin of safety and operating leverage all fall out of it.
