> [!info] How this page works
> Each subject keeps its readings + your notes in its own **Homework** binder (look for `· Homework` in the left menu). This page is the **cross-subject overview**: it lists every homework note by due date so you can see the whole week at a glance. Full readings and answer drafts are never stored on the public site — only a citation and your own notes.
