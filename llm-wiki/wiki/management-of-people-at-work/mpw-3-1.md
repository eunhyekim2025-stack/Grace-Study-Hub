> [!summary] Key takeaways  
- Core drivers (work nature, promotion, supervision, peers, pay, autonomy, recognition) predict satisfaction more than salary alone.  
- Satisfied employees generate OCB, reduce CWB, and boost performance and retention.  
- Nudging, open‑plan design, and flexible workspaces can raise satisfaction but may create new distractions or cultural costs.  
- Aligning personal values with job rewards (value‑congruence) drives long‑term engagement and career clarity.  

## Job Satisfaction Foundations  
- **Job satisfaction**: positive affect toward one’s job, measured by a global “how much do you like your job?” rating and facet scores (pay, supervisor, coworkers).  
> [!info] Global rating predicts engagement and turnover better than many demographic variables.  
- **Attitudes** in OB focus on satisfaction and motivation theories.  

## Key Drivers (correlational evidence)  
| Driver | Relative impact on satisfaction |
|--------|---------------------------------|
| Nature of the work (intrinsic enjoyment) | strongest |
| Promotion opportunities | > pay |
| Supervision quality | strongest after promotion |
| Coworker relationships | ≈ pay |
| Pay | significant but lower |
| Autonomy | important, limited by micromanagement |
| Recognition (feedback, small gestures) | incremental boost |

> [!warning] Over‑emphasizing perks (free meals, nap pods) can mask deeper issues like poor supervision or limited growth.  

## Design, Nudging & Physical Environment  
- **Open‑plan offices**: lower power distance, speed up questions/mentorship; downside = noise, reduced focus.  
> [!warning] Distractions can erode deep work; balance with quiet zones.  
- **Nudging / Choice Architecture**: place healthier foods at eye level, set defaults that steer decisions while preserving freedom.  
> [!info] “Paternalistic libertarianism” – guide without eliminating options.  
- **Google case study** (example): data‑driven people ops, “welcome” interactions boost new‑hire productivity ≈ 50 % in 9 months; amenities (nap pods, treadmill desks) and food nudges cut calorie intake by billions.  

## Behavioral Outcomes  
- **Organizational Citizenship Behavior (OCB)**: discretionary helping, extra effort, improves team performance.  
- **Counterproductive Work Behavior (CWB)**: theft, sabotage, absenteeism, quitting; rises when satisfaction falls.  
> [!info] Satisfied workers → OCB ↑, CWB ↓ → higher overall productivity.  

## Culture, Embeddedness & Risks  
- Deep cultural embedding (e.g., lifelong employment at Google) raises retention but makes layoffs psychologically costly (identity loss).  
- **Physical/social embeddedness**: personalizing workspaces, fostering friendships → stronger attachment, lower turnover.  
- **Micromanagement** is viewed negatively; autonomy is prized when not overridden.  

## Value Alignment & Career Planning  
- **Value‑congruence theory**: satisfaction = fit between job rewards and personal values (money, status, impact). Larger gaps → lower satisfaction.  
- **Self‑concordance**: pursuing goals that match core values sustains effort and success.  
- **Career‑search steps**:  
  1. Define must‑haves, high priorities, deal‑breakers (red‑flags).  
  2. Rank personal values (drag‑and‑drop exercise).  
  3. Match strengths (consistent high‑performance tasks) with values.  
  4. Use structured peer feedback to uncover blind spots.  
- Interview tip: ask about time freedom, supervision style, pressure levels to gauge cultural fit.  

## Practical Exercises  
- **Values‑ranking**: online drag‑and‑drop; reflect individually, then discuss with a partner (infer values from “peak experience”).  
- **Peak‑experience sharing**: describe a memorable achievement without naming values; partner guesses values → self‑awareness boost.  
- **Randomized group work**: promotes diverse interaction, reduces homophily, supports community building.  

## Organizational Metrics  
- **Gallup employee engagement survey**: measures basic needs, meaningful work, recognition, teamwork, quality commitment, growth. Engagement rose from ~10 % (15 yr ago) to ~20 % today; still only 1 in 5 engaged.  

## Key Terms  

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Job Satisfaction | Positive affect toward one’s job; global and facet measures |
| Nudging | Choice‑architecture technique that steers decisions while preserving options |
| OCB (Organizational Citizenship Behavior) | Voluntary, extra-role actions that help the organization |
| CWB (Counterproductive Work Behavior) | Negative actions (theft, sabotage, absenteeism) stemming from dissatisfaction |
| Value‑Congruence | Alignment between personal values and job rewards |
| Self‑Concordance | Pursuing goals that reflect core personal values |
| Open‑Plan Office | Workspace layout that reduces hierarchy but can increase distractions |
