# MPW 1-1 — What an organization is

> [[management-of-people-at-work/index|← Management of People at Work]] · a one-line definition captured from the seminar's opening.

> [!todo] Verify — short AI draft from a class recording
> Only a single line was recorded; check the full textbook wording.

> [!info] Definition (from the textbook)
> **An organization is a consciously coordinated social unit.**

Unpacking the definition:

- **Consciously** — deliberate, intentional; it is designed and managed, not an accident.
- **Coordinated** — roles and efforts are aligned toward shared ends (the reason management exists).
- **Social unit** — it is made of *people* interacting, which is exactly what this course studies.
