---
title: "TEST - Consideration"
created: 2026-07-10
---

Consideration is one of the essential elements needed to form a legally binding contract in Singapore. It refers to something of value that each party gives or promises to the other, often described as the price for which the promise of the other is bought. Key points: consideration must be sufficient but need not be adequate. Consideration must move from the promisee. Past consideration is generally not good consideration, as in Roscorla v Thomas, but under Lampleigh v Brathwait past consideration can be valid if the act was done at the promisor's request. Performance of an existing duty is normally not fresh consideration, as in Stilk v Myrick, but in Williams v Roffey Bros a practical benefit can count. Promissory estoppel can be used as a shield but not as a sword.
